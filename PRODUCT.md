# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS for GitHub Pages. GSAP 3 + ScrollTrigger from cdnjs. No build step. Images always optimized WebP.

## Users

Drivers in Santiago (mainly Recoleta / zona norte) who need maintenance or a repair and want to book fast, mostly from a phone, often after seeing a promo.

## Product Purpose

Single landing page for Nascar Express, an auto repair and maintenance shop (taller mecánico). Success = the visitor contacts by WhatsApp or phone to book, especially on the current weekly offer.

## Positioning

Honest, fast, efficient, experienced neighborhood shop with a guarantee ("Servicio garantizado"); 4.7★ on Google with 417 reviews (Nov 2026).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: Mantención por KM, Cambio de embrague, Frenos, Scanner automotriz, Limpieza de inyectores, Neumáticos y balanceo.
- Booking happens over WhatsApp (+56 9 5217 7645) or phone (+56 2 3282 2490). No backend, no forms.
- Weekly offer changes often; it lives in `data/offer.json` and must be editable without touching HTML.
- Navbar keeps pointing to the existing nascarexpress.cl subpages (Nosotros, Servicios, Blog, Contacto); no subpages are built here.
- Hours: Lun–Vie 08:00–18:00, Sáb 08:00–15:00. Address: Av. El Salto 2367, Recoleta, RM.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo (NASCAR Express, red wordmark with blue slanted stripes), brand blue #0060a7, brand red #c91240.
- Look must stay close to the current site but cleaner, more modern, better animated.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real Google reviews (9 quoted on current site). Google rating 4.7, 417 reviews as of Nov 2026. Use verbatim.
- Assets: logo, white logo, red hatchback photo (assets/img/auto.webp), workshop photo (assets/img/taller-*.webp).
- No prices beyond the current offer's discount; do not invent prices, years in business, or counts.

## Product Principles

- The current offer is the loudest thing after the hero.
- One clear action everywhere: book by WhatsApp.
- Less information, better ordered; nothing duplicated.
